# Body Pull changelog

## 1.0.0 - First release

A new addon: needs a full game restart (not just a /reload) after installing.

- A turn-based dungeon puzzle: you can't push anything, only pull. Step close enough for a pack to see
  you, then lead it back to your party's rug, where Kesk and Wyn finish it off while Brother Aldous
  keeps you standing.
- 40 rooms in four delves (Candlewick Mine, Blackbrine Grotto, Ashveil Crypt, Emberdeep Forge), each
  ending in a boss. Every room is solvable and has a par and a gold step count.
- Enemies with real dungeon rules: aggro rings and sight lines, linked packs, casters whose casts you
  break by stepping out of sight, patrols, ghouls that run, sarcophagi that open when you pass, a
  banshee wail that knocks you back, lava, water you can wade, and a Forgemaster who only moves while
  he can see you.
- Free, unlimited undo; instant restart; a lantern hint; click a tile to walk there. Hover a mob to
  see its ring and rules.
- Bronze, silver and gold medals on every room.
- The daily room: the same room for every player in the world on the same date, kept apart from
  your campaign.
- 13 achievements and 8 shields to carry, earned by clearing delves and collecting medals.
- Scores: your medals for all 40 rooms, and the top ten of every room and of the daily room shared
  between players. Every score is checked by replaying its moves before it counts. Share your
  result to the chat of your choice. Sharing can be switched off.
- Settings in the game, in Esc > Options > AddOns and in NetherSuite's hub (Minigames tab).
- Pauses and steps aside in combat; your run is kept move by move through a /reload.
