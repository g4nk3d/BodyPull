# Body Pull

Version 1.0.0 - Interface 16001 (WoW: Forever 1.60.x) - All Rights Reserved (see LICENSE.txt)

A turn-based dungeon puzzle inside World of Warcraft. You can't push anything: you can only pull.
Lead each pack to your party in as few steps as you can. Part of the Nether addon family; works
entirely on its own.

## What it does

- **40 rooms in four delves:** Candlewick Mine, Blackbrine Grotto, Ashveil Crypt and Emberdeep Forge,
  ten rooms each, every delve ending with a boss. Clearing a room opens the next.
- **Real pulling:** a sleeping mob wakes when you step inside its ring where it can see you; pillars
  and walls block sight. Linked packs wake together. Awake mobs follow you; inside the green party
  ring around your rug, Kesk and Wyn kill them. Brother Aldous heals you while he can see you.
- **New ideas in every delve:** casters (break their sight to stop the cast), tunnel rats and ghouls
  that run, water you can wade but they can't, patrols, sarcophagi, a banshee's wail, lava, and a
  Forgemaster who moves only while he can see you.
- **Free undo and instant restart.** Click a tile to walk there; the walk stops when anything wakes.
  Hover a mob to see its ring, its route and its rules. The lantern gives a hint.
- **Medals:** bronze for a clear, silver at or under par, gold at the best known step count.
- **The daily room:** the same room for everyone in the world on the same date. Its results are kept
  apart from your campaign.
- **Achievements and shields:** 13 achievements, and 8 shields for your tank, earned by clearing
  delves and collecting medals.
- **Scores:** your medals for all 40 rooms, and the top ten of every room and of the daily room,
  shared between Body Pull players over the Nether games' hidden channel and your guild. Every
  score is checked by replaying its moves before it counts. Share your result to chat. Settings >
  "Share scores" turns sharing off.
- **Settings:** in the game's Settings screen, Esc > Options > AddOns > Body Pull and NetherSuite's
  hub when it is installed (Body Pull sits on its Minigames tab).
- The game pauses and steps aside in combat. Your run is saved move by move, so a /reload brings you
  back to the same turn.

## Commands

- /bodypull or /bpull - open or close the game
- /bodypull daily - open the daily room
- /bodypull perf - frame time and memory
- /bodypull debug - errors and missing art
- /bodypull reset - forget this character's Body Pull records (asks twice)
